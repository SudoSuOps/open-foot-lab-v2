What we build

Offloading is geometry, not padding.

A soft insole spreads load. An offloading insert moves it — off the spot that is taking the damage and onto the parts of the foot that can carry it. That is a shape problem, and shape is something you can specify, print, and print again.

Every insert below came out of one file — insert_parametric.scad — driven to different numbers. Nothing here is a stock photo; these are renders of the actual geometry.

Because the spec is kept, a replacement pair is the same pair — not a re-scan and a fresh guess. Printed in TPU, one piece, with an integral groove that captures 3 mm spacer mesh. Not a patient-specific orthosis and not a validated medical device — your clinician decides what your foot needs.
